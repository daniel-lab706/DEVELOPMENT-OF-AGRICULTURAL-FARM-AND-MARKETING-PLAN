# Project Management Plan

## Project Overview

This project focuses on the development of an Integrated Agricultural Farm and Marketing Management System designed to 
improve farm operations and agricultural product marketing in Nigeria. The proposed system aims to provide a centralized 
digital platform for managing farm records, tracking inventory and sales, managing customers, and marketing agricultural 
products online.
The project applies project management principles across the project lifecycle, including scope definition, Work Breakdown
Structure (WBS), schedule planning, cost estimation, stakeholder engagement, risk management, quality assurance, and 
governance. With an estimated budget of ₦730,000, the project aims to reduce manual errors, improve operational efficiency, 
and connect farmers directly with buyers through digital technology.

## Project Objectives

- Develop a centralized farm management system.
- Improve farm record keeping and inventory management.
- Enable farmers to market agricultural products digitally.
- Improve communication between farmers and customers.
- Generate automated reports to support decision-making.
- Reduce operational inefficiencies associated with manual systems

## Skills Demonstrated

This project demonstrates the application of key project management principles in planning the development of an 
Integrated Agricultural Farm and Marketing Management System.

* Project Planning and Scheduling: Developing project schedules, task durations, dependencies, and milestones.
* Scope Management: Defining project boundaries, objectives, and deliverables.
* Budget and Cost Management: Preparing a project budget and allocating costs across activities.
* Risk Management: Identifying potential project risks, assessing their impact, and developing mitigation strategies.
* Stakeholder Management: Identifying stakeholders and planning effective engagement and communication.
* Agile Project Management: Applying Agile principles to support flexibility and continuous improvement.
* Requirements Analysis: Identifying business needs and defining system requirements.
* Work Breakdown Structure (WBS): Breaking the project into manageable tasks and work packages.
* Quality Management: Planning system testing, quality reviews, and performance evaluation.
* Change Management: Establishing processes for reviewing and implementing project changes.
* Project Documentation: Preparing the project charter, scope baseline, schedule, budget, and risk management plan.
* Business Analysis and Problem-Solving: Identifying agricultural business challenges and proposing a digital management solution.
